The Fourth Ended. He Didn't.

Logline:

Seven graduates chasing one last summer high get hunted by a masked zealot who turns their Fourth of July bonfire into a blood-soaked lesson in liberty, leather, and lead.

Synopsis:

A group of newly graduated friends head to a remote cabin to celebrate their last free summer before real life begins. What starts as fireworks and bonfires curdles into a bloodbath when they draw the attention of a masked killer known only as the Cowboy Patriot — a nationalist zealot who dresses the American dream in leather, gunpowder, and old glory.

He doesn't just want them dead. He wants to teach them a lesson about freedom — and he intends to make every one of them pay for taking it for granted.

One by one, the friends are hunted through cabin, barn, and backwoods highway in a deadly game of survival. The survivors must out-think a killer who quotes the founding fathers between kills — before the sun rises on a 5th of July none of them will live to remember.

The Villain

Cowboy Patriot

"Sit down. Shut up. Let me tell you about freedom."

Once a nobody, now a nightmare stitched from old flags and older grievances. The Cowboy Patriot wears a cracked porcelain mask painted like a folk-art founding father, a duster cut from surplus canvas, and spurs that jingle a half-second before the screaming starts.

He doesn't hunt for hunger. He hunts to educate — reciting fireworks-stand patriotism between gunshots and hatchet swings, convinced every kill is a lesson the ungrateful youth of America deserve. Underestimate his drawl. It's the last mistake most of them make.

  • Weapon of Choice Baseball bat
  • Signature Leaves cryptic riddles about the American Dream
  • Motive "Un-American" behavior — his rules, his country
